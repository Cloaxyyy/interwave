Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-05-15

What we store

Interwave is a desktop application. The vast majority of your data — playlists, listening history, settings — lives in a local SQLite database on your computer and never leaves the device.

If you create an account, the managed backend stores only:

  • Your email address and a display name (for sign-in and friend search)
  • Your friend list (so we can show you what they're playing)
  • Snapshots of any playlist you choose to Share (the snapshot includes the playlist name, cover, and the list of YouTube IDs in it)
  • Support tickets you submit, with whatever diagnostic data you opt to include

That's it. We don't track listening history server-side, we don't sell anything, we don't have ads.

Realtime presence

When you have Presence enabled in Settings, your current track is broadcast to friends through a Supabase Realtime channel. Nothing is persisted from this — friends just see what you're listening to right now.

Crash diagnostics

Errors caught in the app are kept locally in your browser's localStorage. They are never sent automatically. If you submit a support ticket and check "Attach diagnostics," the report you send includes them.

Third parties

  • YouTube — used to resolve and stream audio. Subject to YouTube's terms.
  • LRCLIB — fetched anonymously when you open lyrics for a track.
  • Wikipedia + MusicBrainz — fetched anonymously for the "About the artist" card.
  • Supabase — the managed backend that hosts auth, friends, support, and shared playlists.
  • GitHub Releases — checked for the auto-updater and the "What's New" page.

Account deletion

Email support@interwave.cc from the address on your account and we'll wipe everything tied to it.

Contact

Questions? support@interwave.cc or open an issue on GitHub.
